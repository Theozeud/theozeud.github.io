## About

I am a Ph.D. student supervised by [Éric Cancès](https://cermics.enpc.fr/~cances/) and [Mathieu Lewin](https://www.ceremade.dauphine.fr/~lewin/). My research focuses on new formulations, approximations, and numerical methods for Time-Dependent Density Functional Theory (TDDFT) applied to molecules and materials.

My PhD is funded by [CNRS](https://www.cnrs.fr/fr) as part of the [MaQuI](https://www.cnrs.fr/fr/projet-ri2/maqui) project, under the ["Risky and High-Impact Research" (RI)](https://www.cnrs.fr/fr/actualite/la-recherche-du-risque) program. I am a member of the [MATHERIALS](https://team.inria.fr/matherials/) team at [Inria Paris](https://www.inria.fr/fr/centre-inria-de-paris).

## Research interests

- Time-Dependent Density Functional Theory (TDDFT)
- Numerical analysis of electronic structure models
- Mathematical modeling for quantum chemistry and materials science
